Meanwhile, the school needs the doctor’s document, and a benefits office might need proof that another agency already has in its system.

The people working to align all these systems are parents, often people with the least time and cognitive availability to handle the burden.

Political scientists Pamela Herd and Donald Moynihan of the Better Government Lab call this load administrative burden; that burden includes learning all the rules, figuring out how to comply with them, and then handling all the rippling demands that each individual task creates.
