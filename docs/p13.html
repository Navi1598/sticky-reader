Transformers as transport systems

Large power transformers are crucial to transporting electricity over long distances . Power plants – whether coal- or gas-fired, hydropower dams, or solar or wind farms – are often far from the customers who need the electricity that’s generated.

Transmitting power through wires generates heat , which over a long distance dramatically reduces the amount of energy arriving at the far end. To reduce energy losses, power companies use large transformers to increase the voltage. You can think of this like freight being loaded onto large trucks and directed over interstate highways.
