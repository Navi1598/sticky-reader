Pretend the marbles are the clouds. Sometimes, the marbles spin into each other and stick. Other times, the marbles miss each other. In every case, there is a total amount of spin that the marbles share, even if you cannot tell by eye.

Once the clouds spiral into each other, the material inside them collides. This collision squishes the clouds, changing their overall motion and heating them up to the point of glowing. The clouds slow down as they pass through each other and move away, but they can no longer escape each other’s gravitational pull. They continue this spiral dance until they fuse into a larger rotating cloud. The process then repeats for bigger rotating clouds.
