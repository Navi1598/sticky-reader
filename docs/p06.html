Improving precision medicine

Overall, our research shows that DNA variation is very common, and broader genetic testing may help optimize the medications clinicians prescribe to patients. It’s also an example of how big initiatives such as the All of Us Research Program can advance precision medicine .

However, this study doesn’t prove testing would improve outcomes for each of these people. Additionally, genes are only one factor; age, kidney and liver function, and other medications also affect how the body processes drugs .

Still, I believe the direction of the research is clear. Pharmacogenomics can provide potentially relevant benefits for many people who take medications.
