The support of Black voters for El-Sayed was not a given. In the August primary, 62% of Detroit voters chose U.S. Rep. Haley Stevens over El-Sayed. Many pundits predicted it would be difficult for El-Sayed to gain support in Detroit, which is about 75% Black .

After the primary, El-Sayed made overtures by sharing his plans to improve the lives of Black Americans, which included supporting the growth of minority-owned businesses, creating more affordable housing, eliminating food deserts and expanding the Rx Kids cash assistance program nationwide.

About 91.8% of Detroit voters supported El-Sayed in the general election, according to the city’s unofficial results . Detroit City Clerk Janice Winfrey said 209,924 residents voted in the 2026 midterms , up about 21% from the 2022 midterm elections. For the 2024 presidential election, 247,770 city residents cast a ballot.
