This orb-weaver rests on the stabilimentum of its web.

I first became interested in the debate over stabilimentum function because I was unconvinced that one explanation could account for all the evidence. I started out asking whether somebody was wrong. The better question turned out to be why researchers expected one universal answer in the first place.

That remains one of my favorite lessons from animal behavior: A name can be useful, but it can also sneak in an explanation. Once you think you know what something is, you become less likely to watch what it actually does.
