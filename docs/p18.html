US wildfire damage is on pace to triple by mid-century, but there are affordable ways to lower the risk

Virginia Iglesias, Earth Lab Director, University of Colorado Boulder · The Conversation · Nov 03

A fast-moving fire burned hundreds of homes in Spokane, Wash., in August 2026. Mario Tama/Getty Images As temperatures rise and development expands, the number of homes and other structures damaged or destroyed by wildfires across the U.S. is on pace to triple by mid-century , my colleagues and I found in a study just published.

The 2026 wildfire season has been a sharp reminder of that risk and why making homes and properties less vulnerable to wildfires is increasingly crucial. Fortunately, strategies to lower the risk of losing a home to wildfire don’t have to be overly expensive .
