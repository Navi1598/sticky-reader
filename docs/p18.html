Longer term, the U.S. could produce more grain-oriented electrical steel, process more copper and expand its capacity to build large power transformers.

All the ways people want to adapt, improve, expand and modernize the power grid are dependent on this one type of equipment. Without large power transformers, none of the efforts will get very far.

In a sense, leaders face a choice either way: solve these hardware shortages or accept the default consequences of grid congestion, delayed projects and rising costs. Resolving the transformer bottleneck would be what turns that passive reality into active choice.
