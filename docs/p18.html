Shapiro’s political victories didn’t just happen. Over the past four years, he has worked to demonstrate bipartisan competence as Pennsylvania’s chief executive, adopting an expression he uses frequently: “ get sh-t done .”

Now, with Democrats holding the governor’s office and both chambers of the General Assembly, Shapiro told reporters : “We got the power now, the House, the Senate. We have to deliver.”

Shapiro’s approval ratings have remained strong , consistent with his reelection margins. That’s unusual in a state that has swung back and forth between supporting Donald Trump in 2016 , Joe Biden in 2020 and Trump again in 2024 , each time by fewer than 2 percentage points.
