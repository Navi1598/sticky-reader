Special prosecutors would be appointed to investigate abuses of power and violations of law.

A Democrat, Jimmy Carter, would be elected president two years after the massive 1974 midterm victory, and more reform legislation would be passed.

The post-Watergate congressional agenda was long and bold – the war powers resolution, a freedom of information act, the creation of inspectors general, campaign finance reform, a presidential records act and new legislation to regulate foreign intelligence surveillance and the bribing of foreign officials.

These new laws were supposed to stop, or uncover, the kind of wrongdoing that made Nixon an imperial president .
