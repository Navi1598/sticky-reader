Kimberly Rose Clark, Researcher and Visiting Scholar in Psychological and Brain Sciences, Dartmouth College · The Conversation · Nov 12

Residents evacuate their homes after storms battered Santiago, Chile, on Oct. 13, 2026. Floodwater can carry many health threats. Lucas Aguayo Araos/Anadolu via Getty Images As communities around the world shore up their infrastructure in anticipation of one of the most powerful El Niños on record in late 2026 and early 2027, they need to think about rising health risks, too – and include them in early weather warnings.
