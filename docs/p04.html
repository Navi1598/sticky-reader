The repetition gave me something to follow. You can train yourself to do this almost anywhere.

Watch honeybees fly around and see what you notice.

When I take students or my sons to watch honeybees , I tell them not to stare directly at the hive entrance. Instead, sit nearby, look toward the open sky, and relax your gaze. At first you see empty air. Then a bee crosses it. Then another. Within moments the emptiness becomes a busy airport of arrivals and departures.

Nothing new has entered the scene. You have just changed what you are capable of noticing.
