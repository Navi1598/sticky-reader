Microbes produce most of the carbon dioxide, hydrogen and methane, though some of the carbon dioxide is made when digestive juices mix with stomach acid.

You might think you’re too polite to fart, but those bursts of gas make it out anyway – on average, 14-20 times each day.

But wait: These are not the gases that give farts their delightful array of aromas. What makes farts so malodorous is other gas molecules that contain the element sulfur . Pure sulfur has no smell on its own, but when combined with other elements it makes molecules that reek.

One key culprit is hydrogen sulfide , which is famous for smelling like rotten eggs.
