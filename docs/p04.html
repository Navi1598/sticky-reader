Cartoon animals figure prominently as logos for tech companies and products.

Big Tech, in particular, has long embraced a virtual menagerie of cute animals . Silicon Valley has given the world the Firefox Fox, now-defunct Gateway cows, the former Twitter bird, GitHub’s octopus, Mailchimp’s monkey and Hootsuite’s owl. The perceived innocence of creatures can soften how people perceive the consequences of innovation.

Moneymakers

AI companies are pursuing what some researchers have referred to as the “ cute economy ” to make money as well. Feelings generated through cuteness can build brand loyalty and encourage people to keep engaging with the products. Cuteness can function as a marketing strategy, too, as users become emotionally invested in a product. This, in turn, can influence purchases .

Research also shows that people confide in a friendly character more than a plain tool, sharing moods, routines, relationships and preferences.
