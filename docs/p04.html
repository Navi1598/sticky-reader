Fuel is the rescue budget

A servicing spacecraft, like LINK, launches with a finite supply of propellant. Engineers split that fuel supply among the mission’s jobs, from reaching the target and approaching it safely to capturing it and moving it to a new orbit. They also pack some extra fuel in case of surprises.

Think of a tow truck that burns most of its gas getting itself out of a ditch. Even if it reaches a broken-down car, it may not have enough fuel left to tow it home.

That scenario is close to what happened to LINK.
