And in January 2026, the White House Council on Environmental Quality revised the rules around public comments on proposed federal land management actions. Government agencies are required under the National Environmental Policy Act to give the public a chance to comment on rule changes. It is the primary way the public can raise objections, question the thoroughness of the options, or to provide other evidence to help inform policy. The council significantly shortened, or in some cases eliminated, the time period during which the public may submit comments – though that decision has also been challenged in court, saying the Administrative Procedure Act requires the original amount of public comment.
