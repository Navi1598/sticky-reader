To get around that delay, companies that run data centers to power artificial intelligence systems have sought to bypass the grid altogether, by installing dedicated, on-site natural gas power plants to run the computer centers.

That move has caused another supply chain problem . Gas turbines burn at over 2,000 degrees Fahrenheit (1,100 C), which is hot enough to melt standard steel. Turbine blades are manufactured with a complicated process out of very specialized combinations of metal and coated with ceramic heat shields .

As tech companies have placed more and larger orders, the time between ordering a gas turbine and having it delivered has stretched to between three and seven years . And prices in 2027 are expected to be nearly triple what they were in 2019 , due to the increase in demand and still-rare materials.
