Cells are the building blocks of all human tissues and organ systems. How sensitive they are to different environmental factors can greatly affect a person’s health and disease risk.

Thanks to revolutions in stem cell technologies , scientists are now able to model the effects of the environment on many different cell types – including brain cells that are nearly impossible to study in living people without causing irreversible damage to the brain. Instead, my colleagues and I obtain stem cells created from the blood or skin of human donors and convert them to brain cells for our experiments.

Most studies focus on how cells from only a handful of people respond to environmental stressors, mainly because it is quite difficult to conduct experiments
