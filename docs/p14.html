The Democrats lost their majority in the Senate, and 54 seats were flipped in the House of Representatives, making Gingrich the first Republican speaker of the House since Eisenhower lived in the White House.

It was a wipeout election that had not been expected, and it changed the dynamics of Democrat Bill Clinton’s presidency .

The combative Gingrich emerged as a political celebrity; partisanship intensified and gridlock became the new norm. Confrontations over spending led to controversial government shutdowns and the first modern threat to default on America’s debt obligations .
