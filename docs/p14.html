In the end, though, essential services would still almost certainly be affected. Funding for police, fire and emergency medical services is primarily the responsibility of local governments, and, for most counties, they make up the largest line item in their annual expenditures. Reducing property taxes would amount to a direct hit, especially in smaller, less-populated counties.

And if local governments do raise sales tax rates to the extent they can, lower-income Floridians will bear a disproportionate burden as they spend much more of their income on consumption. They’re also far more likely to rent than own a home, so any increased rents would take a bigger toll.
