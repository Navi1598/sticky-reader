The children who deserved a future

I keep thinking about the little children.

Between 1979 and 1981, Atlanta was terrorized by the disappearances and murders of Black children and young adults. Mothers pleaded for answers. Families lived in a city where childhood itself had become dangerous.

The murders unfolded just years after Maynard Jackson’s historic 1973 election as Atlanta’s first Black mayor. His victory announced a new era of Black political power.
