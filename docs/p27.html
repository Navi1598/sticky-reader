The Florida Key deer infestation

Wildlife have received far less attention than livestock during screwworm infestations, with one exception: In July 2016, the New World screwworm was detected in the Florida Keys in an endangered deer population : the Florida Key deer ( Odocoileus virginianus clavium ).
