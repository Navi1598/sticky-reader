American winter that just ended.

South America saw a huge amount of snow, particularly in the central Chilean Andes. In some areas like Portillo, the snow accumulation was the deepest since the current drought began in 2010, and the deepest in the past 70 years north of Santiago at the top of the Limarí River.

The snow was a boon for Chile’s ski resorts. Valle Nevado got over 24 feet (7.3 meters) of snow and extended its ski season, originally scheduled to end in early October, into early December.
