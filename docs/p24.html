Same street, different risks

When these other factors are considered, your home’s true flood risk is far more complex than the map might seem to indicate.

The data shows how two houses on the same street can face radically different risks from flooding , even when located in the same flood zone – and where local governments can help.

One home may be newer, well maintained and covered by flood insurance. Another may be decades older, at the low end of the same street where water runs deeper during a flood, and is in poor repair and uninsured. The same storm would leave them in very different predicaments, with very different paths to recovery.
