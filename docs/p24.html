El Niño storms can cause widespread damage, including mudflows, cutting off transportation routes and leaving residents to try to clean up the mess, particularly in rural areas.

Distance compounds these risks. Many communities around the world don’t have local clinics. Rural Americans live an average of 10.5 miles (17 kilometers) from the nearest hospital , and for 1 in 4 rural residents, the trip takes more than 30 minutes on average – before a single road floods. During Hurricane Harvey in 2017, flooding limited access to Houston’s second-busiest hospital for five days. The most extensive disruptions were in lower-income and minority neighborhoods.
