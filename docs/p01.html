Why do galaxies spin or rotate?

– Swara Dhat, age 13, Thane, India

Spinning is fun, and you can find many ways to do it. From carousels to an office chair, spinning is everywhere – even in outer space.

As astrophysicists , we know that spinning – or rotating – is extremely common in space . The Earth, the Moon, the Sun, the entire Milky Way, and many things inside and outside of it, rotate.
