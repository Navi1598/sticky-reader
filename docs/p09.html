Florida just voted for lower property taxes – and that could mean cuts in government services and new housing construction

Aaron D. Wood, Associate Professor of Economics, University of Tampa · The Conversation · Nov 04

Over 60% of Floridian voters voted to cut property taxes, which has been surging in recent years. AP Photo/Rebecca Blackwell) Many Florida homeowners cheered on Nov. 3, 2026, when more than 60% of voters approved a constitutional amendment to significantly reduce property taxes.

Under the measure, Amendment 3 , a homeowner’s tax break known as a homestead exemption would go from the current US$51,400 to $150,000 in 2027 and increase again to $250,000 in 2028. By shielding more of a home’s assessed value from taxation, this exemption effectively lowers homeowners’ tax bills.
