Another truism, again reaffirmed, is that a president’s approval ratings often are useful in gauging the country’s mood before midterm elections. President Donald Trump’s preelection approval ratings hovered at or below 40% , according to survey data compiled by RealClearPolitics.

Voters cast their ballots at the Bridge of Hope Church in Youngstown, Ohio, on Nov. 3, 2026.

The U.S. war with Iran, launched by Trump, was a major factor propelling the unenviable ratings . The conflict went unresolved while fuel prices surged , bringing pain to consumers.

Those and other problems prompted polling analyst Nate Silver to write that Trump “almost seems to have a death wish in these midterms. And the one superpower he has – turning out low-propensity voters in presidential years, the sorts of voters who apparently are often missed by pollsters – has never really worked when Trump himself wasn’t on the ballot.”

And it didn’t work this time, either, even though throughout the campaign Trump encouraged supporters to pretend his name was on the ballot and turn out to vote.
