The European Space Agency’s ClearSpace-1 is planned for 2029 and aims to capture the Proba-1 satellite, which doesn’t have a docking plate or anything to help ClearSpace-1 capture it, and remove it from orbit.

LINK’s mission showed that a rescue takes more than a robotic arm and enough thrust to reach a target. It also takes a servicer spacecraft that can stay stable, recover from its own faults and keep enough propellant reserved to finish the job. In Swift’s case, the rescuer fell short before the rescue could begin.
