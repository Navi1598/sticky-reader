Insects provide food for other species

Of course, insects are really important food supplies. In terms of sheer numbers and biomass , they dominate nearly every terrestrial and freshwater ecosystem. If you added up the weight of all the insects on Earth , they would vastly outweigh all the wild vertebrates and be on par with the total weight of humans and livestock combined.
