Common global effects of El Nino during the December to February period when it is most pronounced.

NOAA

Disasters can also keep people from getting essential healthcare, disrupt their livelihoods and displace them from their homes and social networks.

The threat of a changing, potentially unsafe environment can also increase anticipatory, long-term distress. The World Health Organization identifies anxiety and post-traumatic stress as mental health consequences of extreme weather-related hazards. We believe mental health counseling services also belong in communities’ preparedness and recovery plans, not as an optional add-on after addressing physical needs.
