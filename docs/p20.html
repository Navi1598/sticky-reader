progressive politics . Historically, Black churches have been more than a place to foster belief. They have also been community-based institutions that fostered social, political and economic justice.

Within Black denominations, men tend to hold senior leadership positions. In his famed book “ The Souls of Black Folk ,” sociologist W.E.B. DuBois observed that the Black preacher was also a community “leader, a politician, an orator, a ‘boss,’ an intriguer, an idealist.”

Women’s role was less public, but no less vital , including social and political organizing. In “ Witnessing and Testifying ,” religion professor Rosetta Ross showed how Black women’s faith and activism inspired each other during the Civil Rights Movement.
