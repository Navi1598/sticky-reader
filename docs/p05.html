Observation has its own traps. Sometimes you can miss behavior because it blends into the background. Other times you see something perfectly but stop asking questions because somebody has already told you what it means.

Orb-weaver spiders offer a good example . Some add conspicuous bands or patches of dense silk to their webs. These structures are called stabilimenta , a name inherited from the old idea that they stabilize or strengthen the web. But they don’t.

Researchers later found evidence for several possible functions: attracting prey, deterring or confusing predators, protecting webs from larger animals, and, in some cases, helping with temperature regulation. Different functions may matter in different species, environments or stages of life.
