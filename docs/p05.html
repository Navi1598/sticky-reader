Gathering glass

Once you’ve adjusted to the chaos of the studio, it’s time to start working.

We have two furnaces in our hot glass studio, where we work with soft glass, so anytime during the school year there is between 700 to 1,200 pounds of glass. It’s kept in the furnace at its molten state (2,200 degrees F or 1,204 degrees C), ready to be gathered and transformed.

Glass artists heat up their glass in a furnace so that it’s molten and easy to mold.
