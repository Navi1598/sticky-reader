Black Michiganders helped push Abdul El-Sayed to victory – but the numbers show he was less popular with this key voting bloc than some other Democratic candidates

Christine Slaughter, Assistant Professor of Political Science, Boston University · Politics + Society – The Conversation · Nov 05

Progressive Abdul El-Sayed’s victory in Michigan’s U.S. Senate race was one of the most-watched contests in the 2026 midterm elections.

As the results for this race came in, I had my eye on one demographic: Black voters. I am a political scientist who studies Black political behavior. My research has been published in numerous journals , as well as in my forthcoming book, “Black Resilience,” which examines Black voters’ political participation.
