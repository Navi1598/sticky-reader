Benjamin Rush, an early public health advocate who led the city of Philadelphia through the yellow fever epidemic of 1793, was the only person with a medical degree to sign the Declaration of Independence.

Since the late 1980s, the number of physicians serving in Congress has increased substantially term over term. My colleagues and I have documented this rise, as well as how physician members of the House have influenced policy.

We found that physician members of the House were more active and effective in enacting health policy than other members.
