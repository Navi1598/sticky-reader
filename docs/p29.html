For instance, Trump’s Jan. 20, 2025, executive order declaring a “ national energy emergency ” has led the Department of the Interior to accelerate its review of proposals for oil and gas drilling, production and refining, and mining, along with other energy-related natural resource development.

A process that used to take one to two years has been reduced to no more than a month . That provides less time for people to review documents, understand what they say and decide how they feel about the proposals. However, 15 states are now suing the Trump administration , saying that fast-tracking violates federal law and seeking court orders to restore the previous process.
