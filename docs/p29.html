This technological optimism has deep roots. In 2004, Princeton scholars Steve Pacala and Robert Socolow proposed the “ stabilization wedge ” approach to addressing climate change. It slices future emissions growth into 15 strategy wedges, each representing a way to cut 1 billion tons of carbon dioxide. Selecting from that menu, government could piece together a suitable emissions reduction strategy.
