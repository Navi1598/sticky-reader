Swift’s reentry comes after a failed rescue mission in the summer of 2026. LINK, a commercial robotic spacecraft sent to raise Swift into a safer orbit, reentered Earth’s atmosphere in September . The machine designed to save a falling satellite burned up first.

As a Ph.D. candidate in aerospace engineering , I know that rescuing satellites is hard . I research how spacecraft approach and capture satellites, including objects that tumble uncontrollably.

Swift’s loss points to a new hazard for the young industry that repairs, refuels and moves spacecraft around in orbit. If something goes wrong, the spacecraft doing the rescuing can become another problem.
