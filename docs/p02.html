Could any of these scenarios have been predicted? The simple answer is not yet – but perhaps soon with the help of biomarkers.

A biomarker is anything measurable in the body that gives clinicians insight into someone’s current or future health. Biomarkers come in many forms and play a critical role in modern medicine by shedding light on the inner workings of the human body.

Many biomarkers are measured during routine procedures at the doctor’s office, including through blood tests and vital signs.

Physiological biomarkers such as heart rate and blood oxygen levels indicate how well your body is functioning.
