They come with a family of brightly colored, globular mascot faces that float across your screen and converse with you. OpenAI says Dots will “bring you work done the way you would do it, sometimes before you even think to ask .”

Startups are making the same bet. The promotional material for Pickle , a personal AI app embodied in a floating, purple, three-dimensional animal that has big, inquisitive eyes, says that people will talk more, and share more, with “something alive you grow attached to that remembers your life and eventually acts alongside you.”

Although cute designs are not inherently sinister, they can make unfamiliar technology feel much more accessible and comforting. Cuteness can also obscure the invasive potential of AI agents, soften how users perceive risks such as extensive data collection and surveillance, and distract people into allowing agents to act on their behalf without clear consent.
