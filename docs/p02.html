But why is that? Why does anything rotate at all in space? To answer this question, we first need to understand how objects spin.

Spinning has laws, too

The amount of spin that an object has is called its angular momentum . It depends on the mass of the object and how that mass is distributed around the object’s center of mass . The center of mass is the location at which an object could be balanced on a point. Have you tried to balance a ruler on your finger? You balance it in the middle, which is the center of mass.

If something is spinning, and its mass and shape stay the same, its spinning speed stays the same. If its mass or the shape changes, its spinning speed also changes.
