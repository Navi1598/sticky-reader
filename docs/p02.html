Different mechanisms are involved, too. You can get better at hearing by being more selective or more consistent . Think of writing the letter “A.” You can practice to make your “A” either more distinguishable from “B” or look more similar to other “A’s” every time you write it. The latter is called internal noise – how consistently your auditory system responds to the same sound over time. The less internal noise you have, the more reliably you can tell similar sounds apart.
