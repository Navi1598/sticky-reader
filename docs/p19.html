Specifically, when women run for office, they motivate young girls to get involved in politics.

Women of color, however, are especially inspiring for young girls. Not surprisingly, they serve as role models for young girls of color, igniting their political interest and even leading them to envision themselves as running for office one day.

But their impact also extends to white girls, who are inspired by seeing these women overcome the double barriers of gender and race.
