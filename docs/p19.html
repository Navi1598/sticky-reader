The 2026 wildfire season began with historically low mountain snowpack , early snowmelt and exceptional warmth across much of the West. Even before the nation’s hottest summer on record intensified drought and dried vegetation, fires were breaking out.

Colorado’s Aspen Acres Fire destroyed hundreds of structures in June. Four firefighters died from injuries suffered when the fast-moving Knowles Fire overtook them near the Colorado–Utah border in July. In August, wind-driven fires swept into neighborhoods in Spokane, Washington , damaging more than 800 homes and forcing tens of thousands of residents to evacuate.

People watch from a police checkpoint as the Aspen Acres fire burns on July 1, 2026, near Pueblo, Colo. State investigators determined that a tree had fallen on a power line, starting the fire that grew to over 100,000 acres and burned more than 300 homes.
