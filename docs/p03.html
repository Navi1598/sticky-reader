We believe that this sleight of hand of marketing high-tech goods in a disarming package may be the greatest power of cuteness, and tech companies appear to be harnessing it to encourage people to adopt AI.

Meta’s Muse AI agent, seen in this advertisement, has a disarming appearance.

Higher stakes

Presenting new technologies as cute is nothing new. Hello Kitty , Labubus , Tamagotchis and other products have famously promoted cuteness to evoke emotional attachment in consumers. They were just collectibles, however, and largely harmless. They couldn’t read an owner’s messages, access an owner’s accounts or act for them. When the cute thing is an AI agent, however, the stakes are much higher.

We have shown how particular ways of talking about technology can influence perceptions of safety and make dangers of technologies less visible . “Cutewashing” can mask privacy concerns and drive adoption by many people, creating a mass market.
