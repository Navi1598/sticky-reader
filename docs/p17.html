Large power transformers are also key to upgrading the existing grid and reducing its vulnerability to large storms or extended outages .

The solutions are both concrete and complicated.

The U.S. Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency has recommended standardizing transformer designs , rather than having each be custom-built. That would allow manufacturers to produce more units more quickly, with an assembly line-style process. It would also allow utility companies to share emergency spares.

The U.S. Department of Energy has recommended deploying what are collectively called “ grid-enhancing technologies .” These methods include sensors that monitor in real time exactly how much power can be transmitted through long-distance wires and advanced software that can find alternative routes by which to distribute electricity from power plants to maximize what’s available to customers. Together, these types of systems can allow the transmission of 15% to 20% more electricity than without them, easing bottlenecks while waiting for new transformers to be delivered.
