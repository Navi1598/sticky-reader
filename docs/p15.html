I recently published a study on this topic in the journal Race and Social Problems with Molly Biddle, who at the time of writing was a data analyst for the Police Accountability Unit at the Defender Association of Philadelphia. In that study, we analyzed Philadelphia Police Department traffic stop records from 2021 through 2024.

We focused on men ages 18-29 stopped for motor vehicle code violations that did not involve an arrest. Among young Black males, approximately 1 in 4 were investigated alongside another young Black man in the car. Among young white males, less than 1 in 20 were investigated with another young white man.

This fivefold disparity is striking, but it does not necessarily establish discriminatory policing.
