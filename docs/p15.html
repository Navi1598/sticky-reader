What these transformers are made of is a big part of the delay.

One key ingredient is what’s known as grain-oriented electrical steel . This is not at all average steel. It is manufactured using precise thermal and mechanical processes that position the steel’s magnetic crystals in a single direction. The crystalline alignment allows magnetic fields to pass through the metal with low energy loss, which is crucial for handling large amounts of electricity. However, only a handful of specialized steel mills worldwide have the technology to produce it. Just one mill is in the U.S.

Also in short supply is copper , which is made into wires that are wrapped around the core of the transformer , typically made of steel, to achieve the correct voltage adjustment.

Custom-made for each location, large power transformers take years to design, build and install.
