U.S. Sen. Chuck Schumer speaks in front of the U.S. Capitol after a boycott of the Senate Judiciary Committee hearing on the nomination of Judge Amy Coney Barrett to the U.S. Supreme Court on Oct. 22, 2020.

Schumer pulled no punches in a speech the day of Barrett’s confirmation vote. Schumer said the day “will go down as one of the darkest days in the 231-year history of the United States Senate.”

He alleged the Republican majority made “a mockery of its own stated principle – that the American people deserve a voice in the selection of Supreme Court Justices – completing the partisan theft of two seats on the Supreme Court using completely contradictory rationales.”
