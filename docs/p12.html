When Nixon resigned over the Watergate scandal in August 1974, Gerald Ford took office. Inflation was 12.3% , and gas prices were still high .

Right before that year’s midterm election, Ford launched the “ Whip Inflation Now ,” or WIN, campaign. But Republicans lost 48 House seats and five Senate seats.

“WIN” didn’t work, and by the 1976 presidential election, gas prices were over 60 cents a gallon – adjusted for inflation to late 2026, that’s $3.49. Ford lost his reelection bid to Jimmy Carter.

Carter’s struggles

In Carter’s first two years in office, starting in 1977, the monthly average price of unleaded gasoline in the U.S. stayed high and even climbed slightly . Inflation hit 9% in 1978 .
