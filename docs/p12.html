Gen Z voters were another important part of the Democratic gains.

In 2026, 66% of voters ages 18–29 supported Democratic House candidates, compared with 30% who backed Republicans. That 36-point Democratic advantage was substantially larger than the 14-point margin in 2024, according to Tufts University.

There was also evidence of voters switching party choices. Among young people who voted for Trump in 2024 and participated in the 2026 election, 16% supported a Democratic House candidate.
