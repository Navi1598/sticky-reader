Another line of evidence now available to scientists is genetic information. Genes, encoded in DNA strands inside every living organism, provide an instruction manual for how to build that creature. Cells copy that DNA and pass it on – a process that’s been going on for billions of years, generation after generation.

This genetic information accumulates slight changes over time, which scientists call mutations. It is the very existence of mutations that allows evolution – how life changes over time – to happen. This process is what generated the diversity of life you see today.
