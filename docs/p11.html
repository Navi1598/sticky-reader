None of this necessarily is to declare that pollsters have resolved their persistent problems in sampling , especially in interviewing hard-to-reach voters. But big polling misses like that in the Texas governor’s race – where Democrat Gina Hinojosa won by double digits, although polls had shown Republican Gov. That outcome has to be heartening for pollsters, an often- beleaguered and much-criticized cohort.
